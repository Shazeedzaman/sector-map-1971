In July 1971 the Bangladesh Forces divided the country into eleven sectors under General M. A. G. Osmani as Commander-in-Chief. Tap a sector on the map to see who commanded it, where its headquarters sat, and which of today's 64 districts fall inside it.

Three brigade forces also operated across sector lines: Z Force under Lt. Col. Ziaur Rahman (1st, 3rd, 8th East Bengal), K Force under Lt. Col. Khaled Mosharraf (4th, 9th, 10th East Bengal), and S Force under Lt. Col. K. M. Shafiullah (2nd, 11th East Bengal). Lt. Col. M. A. Rab served as Chief of Army Staff and Group Captain A. K. Khandker as Deputy Chief of the Bangladesh Forces.
Compiled from the Bangladesh Army sector records, Banglapedia, the Liberation War Museum and Maj. Gen. K. M. Safiullah's Bangladesh at War. Several sectors changed commanders mid-war; both the first and later holders are listed.
